## Code reference

summary: Where each value comes from, by table and note number. {.open}

- **Chapter 9, Table 1:** 53% fill for one conductor, 31% for two, 40% for more than two.
- **Chapter 9, Table 4:** internal area of each raceway type and trade size, with the 40% column.
- **Chapter 9, Table 5:** area of each insulated conductor (THHN/THWN-2 here).
- **Note 4 to the Chapter 9 tables:** a nipple of 24 in. or less may be filled to 60%.
- **Note 7 to the Chapter 9 tables:** when every conductor is the same size, a decimal of 0.8 or more rounds up to the next whole number.
- **Annex C:** the code's own count tables. They're informative and come from the same Table 4 and Table 5 math.

Wire makers' calculators can show a slightly lower fill: they use their own wire diameters, while this calculator uses the Table 5 areas inspectors check.
